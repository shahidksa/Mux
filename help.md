📘 ClearSum User \& Screen Features GuideWelcome to ClearSum—your private, offline wealth and budget tracker. This guide breaks down the core financial logic, calculation engines, and features of each dashboard screen to help you optimize your capital allocation.🏛️ The Top Global Guardrails RowVisible across all core screens, this operational summary row helps you keep track of your cash safety margins in real-time.🛡️ Safety Floor: A user-defined, non-negotiable cash reserve kept in your primary checking or wallet account. This money is never touched by automation engines and serves as your baseline emergency fallback.🔒 Capital Shield: A secondary buffer allocation used to isolate funds for mandatory upcoming short-term liabilities (e.g., subscription bills or rent protection).🟩 Surplus: The amount of unassigned, flexible cash left over after your safety buffers are fully subtracted from your total liquidity pool.Formula: Surplus = (Total Income - Total Expenses) - (Safety Floor + Capital Shield)Example: If your net cash is $11,650.00, your Safety Floor is $36.00, and your Shield is $5.40, your available Surplus is exactly $11,608.60.📈 Wealth Efficiency: A metric tracking how effectively your capital is being routed into active savings goals rather than sitting unallocated. A score of 100%+ (Optimal) means your surplus liquid cash is safely positioned to generate long-term momentum.📊 Screen 1: The Main Dashboard1. If you spend money on standard expenses mid-week, the engine automatically scales down its next transfer to ensure it never over-sweeps your bank account or violates your Safety Floor.⚙️ Screen 3: Settings \& Guardrail IsolationConfigure your system thresholds, manage active multi-currency conversion frameworks (e.g., seamless USD to PKR dynamic translations), and set up automation cooldown intervals.⏱️ Protected Auto-Sweep Cooldown GuardrailsTo prevent automated transfers from looping or compounding unprompted, ClearSum operates a multi-frequency safety ledger:Daily Mode: Runs once upon activation or interval tick, then enforces a strict cooldown lock until midnight.Weekly Mode: Truces once, then enforces a strict 7-day cooldown lock measured down to the millisecond.Monthly Mode: Runs once, then locks itself until the next calendar month and year register on the system clock.UI Cycling Protection: Toggling between frequencies in the settings panel updates your visual selections but looks up historical timestamps first. If a frequency was already completed within its calendar window, the engine refuses to issue out-of-turn duplicate transfers, fully protecting your wallet balance.
